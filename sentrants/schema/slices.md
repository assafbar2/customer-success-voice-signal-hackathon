# Audience slices

Independent margins lie. “18% founders” × “38% US” × “28% age 23” is not how many US founders are 23.

Two lists, merged into `sentrants mix` / `sentrants slices`:

1. **Covering map** — geo × shop, disjoint, sums to 100%. This *is* the audience.
2. **Callouts** — joints a Sentry person actually says out loud. They overlap.

Your example: **US founder, 23** is a callout, not a census row.
