# Story

**Sentrants.** Sentry + ants. There are two thousand of them. They are people. They stay. You can talk to any of them. They get older in the product: they try it, they live in it, they get pissed, they leave — or they don’t.

You run the place. You can ask them, tell them, change what they pay, ship something, break something, take something away, jump a month, or rewrite who is even here.

How they hatch and keep living: [LIFE.md](./LIFE.md).

## The path (easy to follow)

Four camps. That’s the whole life.

```text
trying  →  in  →  pissed  →  gone
              ↑_______|
```

- **Trying** — just put Sentry on it. Bootcamp project, founder on day three, free plan.
- **In** — this is how they debug. Paying or not. Turning on Replay or Seer Autofix is still *in*, just richer.
- **Pissed** — quota email, noisy alerts, we broke their SDK, we moved the buttons, the invoice jumped, we turned Seer auto-on and it opened PRs they didn’t want.
- **Gone** — they left. Still on the floor, in the graveyard. You can ask why.

Pissed can calm down and walk back to *in*. Gone stays gone unless you rewrite reality.

Growing is not a fifth camp. You see it as plan going up and more of the [portfolio](./schema/portfolio.md) switched on. Same person, still *in*.

## Moves (what we actually do to them)

A move hits a **portfolio id** — `seer.autofix`, `replay.mobile`, `logs`, `sdk.python` — not a brand sticker.

| Move | Real example |
| --- | --- |
| **Ask** | “If Autofix opened PRs on its own, would you leave it on?” They walk. They can forget. |
| **Tell** | Quota email. Changelog. In-app banner. A tweet they saw. “Seer will review your PRs.” |
| **Price** | Plan prices, quotas, PAYG, “Logs are billed now”, “Seer is $40/contributor.” |
| **Ship** | Something they didn’t have yesterday: Logs, Snapshots, `seer.review`, `seer.mcp`. |
| **Break** | SDK init deprecated. API gone. Source maps pipeline changes. Their CI goes red. |
| **Take** | Sunset. Gated to Business. Replay off the free plan. `seer.auto` exists and then it doesn’t. Cheese moved. License. |
| **Jump** | A month later. Renewals, noise, they adopt MCP or they stew. |
| **Rewrite** | “Corporate India is 22%, not 18%.” You changed the swarm, not their memories. |

**Ask vs the rest:** Ask can be a rehearsal (they don’t remember). Everything else happened to them.

**Jump:** we need it. If time never passes, they only change when you poke them, and that’s a survey panel again. It’s a button that says **a month later**.

**Rewrite** is not Jump. Jump: they lived. Rewrite: you cheated the census (spawn/retire a crowd). Don’t drag a living SaaS person into self-hosted.

## The swarm *is* the audience

Two thousand. All of them walk. Sized so a small real slice is still a crowd. After a move the room talks. Walk up to anyone and they’re a full person — they hatched with a name, a grain of voice, and a reason they installed Sentry.
